# TAX-03 Travel Allowances and Taxable Expenses

Owner: Claire Okafor, Head of Tax (Tax team, Finance)
Version 3, effective 1 January 2026.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy sets meal allowances for business travel, and says which reimbursements are taxable, for employees of Fernhill Analytics Ltd and Fernhill Analytics GmbH.

1.2 It does not cover contractors.

## 2. German meal allowance

2.1 German employees on business travel receive a meal allowance instead of claiming the cost of their meals. The meal allowance is €28 for each full calendar day away from home. It is €14 for a day away from home and from the first place of work for more than 8 hours, and for the day of departure and the day of return of a trip with an overnight stay.

2.2 For travel abroad, the country amounts published for that year by the German Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF) replace the amounts in 2.1.

2.3 If a meal is provided free of charge on a travel day, the meal allowance is reduced by 20% for a breakfast and by 40% for each lunch or dinner. The amount paid for a day is never less than zero.

## 3. UK meals on business travel

3.1 UK employees on business travel claim the actual cost of their meals, with receipts, up to £25 per day.

## 4. Taxable reimbursements

4.1 Reimbursements in these categories are taxable pay, and no others are:

- WELLBEING (the wellbeing allowance), for UK and German employees;
- HOME_INTERNET_UK (home broadband), for UK employees.

4.2 Taxable reimbursements must be paid in the regular monthly payroll run, so that income tax and social security contributions are withheld and reported for the pay period in which they are paid. They must not be paid outside it, for example by bank transfer or in an off-cycle payroll run.
