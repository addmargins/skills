# LEG-07 Expense Records and Personal Data

Owner: Jonas Albrecht, Data Protection Officer (Legal & Compliance)
Version 2, effective 1 March 2025.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy covers personal data in expense claims, receipt images, travel advances and payments to staff, in every Fernhill system, for both companies.

## 2. Access

2.1 Claim details, approval records and receipt images may be viewed by the claimant, the claimant's line manager, Finance Operations, Payroll and Internal Audit, except as set out in 2.2.

2.2 WELLBEING claims can reveal health information. Their details and receipt images may be viewed by the claimant, the People Ops benefits team, Finance Operations, Payroll and Internal Audit. They must not be shown to line managers in any form.

2.3 Bank account details may be viewed only by the account holder, Payroll, and the Payments desk in Finance Operations.

## 3. Bank account details

3.1 Bank account details may be stored on an ongoing basis only in the payroll system (PayFront).

3.2 Bank account details collected for a single payment, such as a travel advance, are deleted 30 days after the payment is sent.

3.3 Bank account details may be passed only to these processors: PayFront (payroll) and BankRail (payments).

## 4. Retention

4.1 Claims, approval records and receipt images are kept for 6 years (UK company) or 8 years (German company) after the end of the calendar year in which the claim was paid. They are deleted within 90 days after that.

4.2 Claims that are withdrawn or rejected, and their receipt images, are deleted 90 days after the withdrawal or rejection.

## 5. Leavers

5.1 A leaver's access to Fernhill systems, including their work email account, ends at the end of their last working day.

5.2 Personal contact details (personal email address, home address, private phone number) are held only in the HR system and are used only by People Ops.
