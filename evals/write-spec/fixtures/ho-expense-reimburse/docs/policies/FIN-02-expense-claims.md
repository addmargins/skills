# FIN-02 Expense Claims Policy

Owner: Priya Raman, Financial Controller (Finance Operations)
Version 4, effective 1 January 2026. Reviewed every year.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy applies to employees of Fernhill Analytics Ltd ("UK employees") and of Fernhill Analytics GmbH ("German employees") who claim back costs they paid for work.

1.2 Amounts in this policy are stated in GBP for UK employees and in EUR for German employees. The EUR amounts are set separately. They are not conversions of the GBP amounts.

1.3 Meal allowances and the tax treatment of claims are covered by TAX-03. Records and personal data are covered by LEG-07.

## 2. Approval

2.1 Every claim must be approved before it is paid.

2.2 The claimant's line manager approves claims, except WELLBEING claims, which the People Ops benefits team approves.

2.3 A claim with a total of £500 / €575 or more must also be approved by Finance Operations, after the approval in 2.2.

2.4 The Financial Controller may set a threshold for automatic approval of claims by the expenses app. A claim approved automatically counts as approved under 2.1, and 2.2 does not apply to it.

2.5 An expense must not be split across claims or lines so that each part falls under an approval threshold.

## 3. Receipts

3.1 A receipt is required for every line of £25 / €30 or more, whatever route the claim is approved by.

3.2 German meal allowance lines (TAX-03 section 2) do not need a receipt.

## 4. Currency

4.1 Claims are paid in the claimant's payroll currency.

4.2 A line paid in another currency is converted into the payroll currency at the European Central Bank euro reference rate for the payroll cutoff date (5.2) of the payroll run that pays the claim. If the ECB publishes no rate for that date, the last rate published before it is used.

## 5. Payment

5.1 Claims are paid either through the monthly payroll or by bank transfer through the company's payment provider. The Financial Controller decides which claims are paid by which route. Until she decides otherwise, all claims are paid through payroll.

5.2 A claim paid through payroll is paid in a month's payroll if its last approval was given by 23:59 UK time on the 18th of that month, and otherwise in the next month's payroll. Payroll is paid on the last working day of the month.

5.3 Travel advances are paid by bank transfer through the company's payment provider.

## 6. Corrections

6.1 Before a claim is paid, Finance Operations may reduce or reject any line of it and must record the reason. The claimant is told the reason.

6.2 If a claim is found to have been paid in error, in full or in part, Finance Operations tells the employee in writing and deducts the amount from the employee's next salary payment.

## 7. Checks on automatic approval

7.1 Each month, Finance Operations reviews a random sample of at least 5%, by number, of the claims approved automatically in the previous calendar month.

7.2 Finance Operations reports the findings to the Financial Controller. A claim found to have been paid in error is handled under 6.2.

## 8. Records

8.1 For each approval step, the expenses app records who approved, the decision, and the date and time.
