# Attendance and Absences

Board Policy 5113, Merrow Valley School District
Owner: Dr. Howard Pruitt, Director of Student Services, Office of Student Services
Adopted August 1, 2022. Last reviewed August 4, 2026.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy sets how schools record attendance, what counts as an absence or a tardy, how a guardian explains an absence, and how attendance records are corrected.

1.2 Times in this policy are in the local time of the student's school.

## 2. Recording attendance

2.1 Middle and high schools record attendance for each class period. Elementary schools record it for the morning session and for the afternoon session.

2.2 The teacher records attendance within the first 15 minutes of each period or session.

2.3 Teachers use these codes: P (present), T (tardy), A (absent), and SA (school activity).

2.4 A student who arrives after a period or session starts, but within its first 15 minutes, is tardy (T). A student who arrives later than that is absent (A) for that period or session, even if they attend the rest of it.

2.5 A student away from class on a school-sponsored activity, such as a field trip, an athletic competition, or an academic contest, is present for attendance purposes and is recorded SA.

2.6 A student who arrives after the first bell signs in at the front office and takes a pass to class. A student who leaves during the day signs out at the front office.

## 3. Reasons for absence

3.1 The attendance clerk in each school's front office records the reason for a student's absences on a day with one of these codes: EX (excused), UV (unverified: no guardian has given a reason yet), or UX (unexcused).

3.2 An absence is excused for illness, a medical or dental appointment, a death in the family, religious observance, or a required court appearance. Other reasons are unexcused.

3.3 The reason code applies to every period or session the student missed that day.

3.4 A student is absent for the day when absent from every period or session that day. A student absent from some but not all of them has a partial-day absence.

## 4. Explaining an absence

4.1 A guardian explains an absence by calling the school's attendance line, sending a signed note, or emailing the school's attendance address. A guardian may do this before the absence.

4.2 A guardian must explain an absence by 11:59 p.m. on the second school day after it. An absence still coded UV after that becomes UX.

## 5. Corrections

5.1 A teacher may correct their own attendance marks until 11:59 p.m. on the next school day.

5.2 After that, only the attendance clerk may change a mark or a reason code, and the clerk records why. The clerk may do so up to 10 school days after the absence.

5.3 A change more than 10 school days after the absence needs the principal's approval.

## 6. Truancy notices

6.1 State law requires the school to notify a parent or guardian in writing when a student has three unexcused absences in a school year. The attendance clerk mails this truancy notice within five school days of the third unexcused absence.

6.2 After five unexcused absences in a school year, the school invites the family to an attendance conference.
