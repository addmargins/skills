# Student Records Privacy

Board Policy 5125, Merrow Valley School District
Owner: Daniel Okafor, Records and Privacy Officer, Office of General Counsel
Adopted June 12, 2023. Last reviewed August 4, 2026.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy covers information from a student's education record, including attendance, grades, discipline, and health information, in any form. It applies to the messages the district sends as well as to records released on request.

1.2 In this policy, "parent" includes a legal guardian. A parent holds educational rights for a student when Ledger records them as holding them.

## 2. Who may receive information about a student

2.1 The district discloses information from a student's record only to:
- (a) district staff who need it for their work with the student;
- (b) a parent who holds educational rights for the student;
- (c) a foster parent with whom the student lives, who acts as the student's parent; and
- (d) others, with the written consent of a parent who holds educational rights, or as 2.4 and section 3 allow.

2.2 A parent's right to receive information about their child does not depend on having custody of the child.

2.3 A person named in a court order on file at the school that limits their access to the student's records, or their contact with the student, receives no information about the student from the district.

2.4 The district may disclose information from the record of a student in foster care, without consent, to the caseworker at the state or county child-welfare agency who is responsible for the student's case. The district's foster care point of contact, Leah Brennan (Office of Student Services), arranges these disclosures.

2.5 Relatives, caregivers, and emergency contacts who are not covered by 2.1(b) or 2.1(c) receive information about the student only with the written consent of a parent who holds educational rights.

## 3. Students aged 18 and over

3.1 On a student's 18th birthday, the rights this policy gives parents pass to the student.

3.2 From then on, the district discloses information from the student's record to a parent only:
- (a) with the student's written consent;
- (b) when the parent gives the school proof that they claimed the student as a dependent on their most recent federal tax return; or
- (c) when the principal decides, for that student at that time, that a health or safety emergency requires telling a parent.

3.3 The student may withdraw consent given under 3.2(a), in writing, at any time.

## 4. Information in messages

4.1 A text message or email about a student contains only what its purpose needs. It never contains health information or the reason for an absence.

## 5. Records of disclosures

5.1 For each disclosure of information from a student's record to anyone other than district staff, including a parent, the district keeps a record of the date, the recipient, and the information disclosed.

5.2 The district keeps each disclosure record for as long as it keeps the student's record: until five years after the student leaves the district.

5.3 A parent who holds educational rights, or a student aged 18 or over, may see the student's disclosure records by asking the Records and Privacy Office.
