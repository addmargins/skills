# Family Communications

Board Policy 1112, Merrow Valley School District
Owner: Renee Castellanos, Director of Communications, Office of Communications and Community Engagement
Adopted January 9, 2024. Last reviewed July 22, 2026.

## 1. Scope

1.1 This policy covers messages that the district and its schools send to families by phone call, text message, email, or mail. An automated message is one that Ledger or another district system composes and sends, including when a staff member starts the sending.

1.2 A message about an individual student goes only to people whom the Student Records Privacy policy (BP 5125) allows to receive what it says.

## 2. Who receives messages about a student

2.1 Messages about an individual student are sent to the primary contact of the household in which the student resides.

2.2 Any other parent or guardian of the student may ask the school to send them these messages as well. The school adds them if BP 5125 allows.

2.3 Messages to a whole school or to the whole district, such as closure notices, go to every parent and guardian with a phone number or email address on file.

## 3. Language

3.1 Messages are sent in the family's preferred language when it is one of the district's supported languages: English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Arabic, and Haitian Creole. Otherwise they are sent in English.

3.2 Language Services, in the Office of Multilingual Learners, translates the templates for automated messages. Messages to families are not machine translated.

## 4. Quiet hours

4.1 Automated messages are not sent between 9:00 p.m. and 7:00 a.m. in the local time of the student's school. An automated message that falls due during these hours waits until 7:00 a.m.

4.2 Emergency messages are exempt from 4.1. An emergency message is one about a school closure, a lockdown, a shelter-in-place, or an evacuation.

## 5. Consent and opting out

5.1 The district sends text messages only to mobile numbers whose owner has agreed to receive them. Agreement given on the enrollment form or the annual update form counts.

5.2 When someone replies STOP to a district text number, the district sends no more text messages to that number until they reply START. Replying STOP does not stop email or phone calls.

5.3 Families may opt out of newsletters and event reminders. They may not opt out of emergency messages or of the truancy notices that state law requires (BP 5113, section 6).

## 6. Records of messages

6.1 For each message the district sends through a messaging provider, the district keeps the message text, the recipient's name and phone number or email address, and the delivery status for 90 days after sending, and then deletes them. No copy or summary that identifies the recipient or what the message said is kept after that.

6.2 The 90-day limit is also a term of the district's contracts with its messaging providers.

## 7. Content

7.1 A message about an individual student names the student by first name and school, and says how to reach the school.
