# POL-FIN-007 Deposits and no-show fees (test copy)

## 1. Scope
1.1 This policy applies to bookings for which the patient paid a deposit.

## 4. Patient cancellations
4.1 A cancellation up to one day before the appointment gets the whole deposit back.
4.2 A later cancellation up to 3 hours before gets 50% back.
4.3 A cancellation after that gets nothing back.
